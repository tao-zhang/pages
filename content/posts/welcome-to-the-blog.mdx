If you found this through a search for something specific — an SEO
checklist, a content calendar template, a "how do I actually do this"
question — welcome. That's exactly who this blog is for.

## What we write about

Three things, on rotation:

- **SEO fundamentals** — on-page basics, technical concepts explained in
  plain language, and the small habits that compound into rankings over
  time. No jargon for jargon's sake.
- **Content operations** — the systems and templates that make a
  consistent publishing cadence actually sustainable, whether you're a
  team of one or five.
- **How we're building this** — a running account of standing up a
  content operation from zero: what we tried, what worked, and what we'd
  do differently. Our first growth-strategy post is queued for review now.

## Who it's for

Founders, solo marketers, and small teams who need SEO and content advice
that assumes limited time and no dedicated budget for tooling — not an
agency audience with a full stack of paid platforms already in place.

## What to expect

Four new posts a week, each one built around a specific search intent
rather than written to hit a word count. If a post here doesn't answer the
question that brought you to it, that's a bug — tell us.
